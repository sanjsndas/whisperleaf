Cookies Policy

Just like a garden marker helps you remember where you planted your seeds, cookies help us remember your preferences — and we use them with care.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our site, and improve your experience. Think of them as little plant labels — they help us keep track of what matters to you.

🌿 Our approach: We use only the cookies we truly need. We don't use intrusive tracking, and we never sell your data. Your privacy matters to us as much as healthy soil matters to a garden.

How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: To keep the website functioning properly (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences).
  • Performance: To understand which articles are most popular and how readers navigate the site.
  • Preferences: To remember your settings, such as your preferred reading mode.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies that follow you across the web.

Your choices

You have full control over cookies. You can:

  • Accept all cookies (recommended for the best experience).
  • Decline non-essential cookies while keeping essential ones.
  • Change your preferences at any time using the buttons below.

Your preference will be saved for this browser.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to manage cookie settings. You can usually find these in your browser's "Settings" or "Preferences" menu. Here are links to the most common browsers:

Third-party cookies

We occasionally embed content from third parties (e.g., YouTube videos or Instagram posts). These services may set their own cookies. We do not control these, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.
