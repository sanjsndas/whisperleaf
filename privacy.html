    Privacy Policy

    Your data is like a seedling — handled with care, respect, and transparency. Here’s how we tend to it.

    Our commitment

    At Whisperleaf Garden Stories, we believe that privacy is the soil of trust. This policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep our practices simple, secure, and in harmony with your rights.

    🌿 In short: We collect only what we need to serve you better. We never sell your data. We never share it unnecessarily. And we’re always happy to answer your questions.

    Information we collect

    We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as:

    • Contact details — name, email address, and any message you send via our forms.
    • Newsletter subscriptions — email address and preferences.
    • Comments and submissions — stories, essays, or garden notes you share with us.
    • Usage data — anonymous analytics to understand how our readers interact with the site (e.g., pages visited, time spent).

    We do not collect sensitive personal data unless you explicitly provide it (e.g., in a story submission).

    How we use your information

    We use your data to:

    • Respond to your inquiries and messages.
    • Send you occasional newsletters (only if you’ve opted in).
    • Improve our content and reader experience.
    • Process submissions and collaborations.

    We never use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that affects you.

    Cookies & analytics

    We use minimal cookies to enhance your experience. These include:

    • Essential cookies for site functionality.
    • Performance cookies (anonymous) to understand page popularity.

    You can manage your cookie preferences at any time. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

    Data security

    We treat your data with the same care we give to our garden beds. All information is stored securely, and access is limited to essential team members. We use industry-standard encryption and regularly review our practices.

    Your rights

    You have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you.
    • Request correction or deletion of your data.
    • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
    • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    To exercise these rights, simply contact us — we’ll respond within 2–3 business days.

    Third-party links

    Our articles may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices — we encourage you to read their policies.

    Updates to this policy

    We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We’ll always post the latest version here, with the effective date below.

    Effective date: 1 March 2026 · Last reviewed: March 2026

    If you have any questions, please reach out to us at privacy@whisperleaf.com.
